Who we are

Independent
by design

Registered and based in Dubai, we operate internationally, bringing capability from Europe and Asia into Gulf programmes and taking regional clients out to international supply. We are appointed by the client and we act in the client's interest. We hold no manufacturing interest and no stock, so our incentive is the programme reaching completion, not a unit sold. Partner relationships are disclosed, never implied.

Evidence before momentum

Scope, price and responsibility are agreed in writing before work starts. Verbal enthusiasm is not treated as a commitment, and neither is a customer list treated as proof of corporate structure.

Built for this market

Gulf procurement runs on relationships and on precision in equal measure. We operate in both registers, and we translate between them for clients arriving from outside the region.

One point of coordination

Complex programmes fail in the gaps between parties. We own the middle ground, so the client has one number to call and one person accountable for the schedule.

Assembled around the brief

Every programme is led by the directors you meet, with a specialist team built around the work itself: consultants, designers and manufacturers drawn from a qualified network and engaged project by project. The capability fits the brief rather than the brief fitting a fixed structure.

Capability

What we do

Six service lines, each one within our registered scope of activities. We work inside that scope, and we say so plainly when a brief needs a qualified engineer, a manufacturer or a law firm.

01

Project and programme management

End to end ownership of the plan: scope definition, supplier coordination, milestone tracking, risk registers and reporting. We chair the meetings, chase the actions and keep the schedule honest.

02

Commercial representation and market access

Representing international manufacturers and technology holders into the GCC, and representing regional clients into international supply. Introductions, positioning, negotiation support and relationship management under a written mandate.

03

Design services coordination

Managing the design workstream between the client, the design house and the manufacturer. Brief control, revision control, and translation between commercial intent and technical instruction.

04

Marketing management

Positioning, brand architecture, launch planning and collateral for industrial and premium consumer propositions entering the region.

05

Public relations management

Stakeholder communication, announcement planning and message discipline for launches, partnerships and sensitive commercial situations.

06

Media monitoring and market intelligence

Structured monitoring of competitors, counterparties and category movement, delivered as briefings a decision can be made from.

Sectors

Where we work

We concentrate where technical complexity meets commercial ambition, and where a programme will fail without someone owning the middle ground between the two.

Automotive and mobility

Low volume and specialist vehicle programmes, motorsport adjacent projects, EV and platform sourcing, rolling chassis and powertrain supply coordination, aftermarket and personalisation.

Advanced manufacturing

Additive manufacturing, precision components, tooling and prototype supply chains, including bringing international capacity to regional programmes.

Composites and lightweighting

Carbon fibre and structural composite parts for automotive, marine and architectural applications, from prototype through to repeat supply.

Premium consumer and hospitality

Brand introduction, distribution structuring and trade activation for premium products entering the GCC market.

A note on scope

Apex Drive does not manufacture or hold stock, and does not itself perform engineering design or certification. Those capabilities are delivered through our partner manufacturing and engineering companies, with Apex Drive appointed as the client's single point of coordination across the programme.

Method

How we work

Four stages. Each one ends in a written output, so the client always knows what has been decided and what is outstanding.

01

Define

We establish the commercial objective, the constraints and the decision makers, then write it down. Most programmes fail here, not later.

02

Qualify

We identify and verify the counterparties. Corporate standing, capability and track record are checked separately.

03

Structure

Commercial terms, responsibilities and protections are agreed and documented before activity begins.

04

Execute

We run the programme against milestones, report on a fixed cadence and escalate early, with a recommended course of action.

Engagement models

Retained advisory
A fixed monthly fee for continuing programme oversight and market representation.
Project mandate
A defined scope, a defined deliverable and a fixed fee, with milestone payments.
Representation mandate
Commission based appointment to represent a manufacturer or brand in defined territories, with agreed protection on introduced accounts.
